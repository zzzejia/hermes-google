Hermes Google privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026.

Who uses it

Hermes Google is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: reads events and creates, changes or deletes events when the owner asks.
  • Gmail: reads messages and saves drafts. It has no ability to send email.
  • Google Drive: searches and reads files, and creates documents when the owner asks.

How data is handled

  • Data is fetched on demand to answer the owner's own requests and is shown only to the owner.
  • The sign-in token is stored only in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
